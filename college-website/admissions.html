Admission Process

Ready to begin your academic journey? Follow these simple steps to apply for admission at Techno Valley College.

1

Choose Your Course

Browse our courses page and select the program that matches your career aspirations.

2

Check Eligibility

Review the eligibility criteria for your chosen course and prepare required documents.

3

Submit Application

Fill out the online application form with accurate details and upload documents.

4

Admission Confirmation

Receive your admission offer letter and complete the enrollment formalities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category)
  • Age between 17 and 25 years at the time of admission
  • Transfer certificate and migration certificate (if applicable)

Admissions for the 2026-27 academic year are now open. Apply before July 31, 2026 to secure your seat.
